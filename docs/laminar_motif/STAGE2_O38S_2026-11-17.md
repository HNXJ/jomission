- Sampler: independent Bernoulli per ordered pair (JaxFNE `connectivity.py` 581-640, called at 1486), so the count
  is exactly Binomial(N_pre·N_post, p). There are no autapses (disjoint populations) and no multi-edges (421 511

- Dependence, missed in the first diagnostic: with jax 0.10.2 (`threefry_partitionable`), arms at one seed share the
  rule's random stream. The reviewer verified that a 5785×404 uniform draw is the exact prefix of the 5785×518 draw,

  within a seed is 0.178, and across seeds within an arm 0.919. e_pv_S z for seed 1 over the six arms is 1.67, 2.89,
