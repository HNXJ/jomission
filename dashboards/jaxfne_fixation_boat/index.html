Jaxfne Cortical Fixation Boat Task

A boat is steered toward a target by a laminar spiking column. Each area is a clone of the same per-layer configuration (L1–L6 with E, PV, SST and VIP cells; L1 has no E cells by default). Haptic coupling α sends the boat distance into the E cells of every area: 0 is off, 1 is the maximum. This is a reduced current-based LIF model in the browser, not the jaxfne 0.5.8 engine, and it is not calibrated.

All parameters (JSON)

Every model and task parameter, in one object. Missing keys take the default; unknown keys and out-of-range values are rejected with a message and the running network is kept. Apply parameters rebuilds the network and resets the boat. Sliders change α, cue, tonic and noise live; neurons per column, areas and seed take effect on Apply network. Unapplied text in this panel is kept when a slider moves; presets and Reset overwrite it. DT = 0.5 ms and the 2000 Hz sample rate are fixed in the code.

Defaults, unless changed in All parameters (the connectivity is the routes table there). Connectivity per area: within a layer, E→E (6 inputs), E→PV (18), E→SST (18), E→VIP (12), PV→E inhibitory (4), SST→PV inhibitory one way (4), VIP→SST inhibitory (4, assumed; not in the brief). Layers with no E cells (L1) take E input from L3 (the layer index 2 E population, as in the first version). Between areas, L5 E of area k feeds L2 E of area k+1, and L5 E of area k+1 feeds L6 E and L1 VIP of area k. Motor groups are the first E cells of L5 in the top area (4 groups of motorPerDir cells: +x, −x, +y, −y), decoded every control interval and applied one interval later, as in the fixture. Defaults are not calibrated. Measured headless, 60 s per run, seed 1, 240 neurons per column, 2 areas: distance only (α 0, cue 0) gives e ≈ 3 at 60 s, and haptic α 1 alone drifts further (e ≈ 4.8 at 60 s). A directional cue of 0.2 holds the boat within e ≈ 0.1 with 100% time within 0.5 of the target from 40 s onward, with or without haptic α 0.5. The cue is a labelled addition, not the frozen spec. Fixation is the share of the last 30 s with e ≤ 0.5; before 30 s have passed it is the share since the run started (the stat label shows the window in use).
