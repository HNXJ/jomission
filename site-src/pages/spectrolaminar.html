Spectrolaminar motif from cell densities

Draft for review. Not published. This page holds method and status only; it shows no results.

Question

Does one column per cortical area, built from that area's laminar cell-type densities and wired by declared rules only, reproduce the spectrolaminar LFP motif: gamma power above alpha–beta, with a depth profile that follows the densities?

Method

  1. Densities: PV, CB, CR and NG cell densities and LFP relative power from LANSA (Mulvey et al.), 11 macaque areas, 21 depth bins.
  2. One JaxFNE column per area, 1050 Izhikevich neurons, with E, PV, SST and VIP fractions per depth bin taken from that area's densities.
  3. Canonical type-to-type wiring, shared by all areas. Connectivity is explicit; the engine's default all-to-all graph is off.
  4. Drives set within a physiological firing regime. The circuit is chosen in that regime and held out by area.
  5. Simulated LFP is analysed with the paper's PCA and PLSR pipeline, ported to NumPy.
  6. MEG and EEG come from the same runs: a column dipole, then a head model.
