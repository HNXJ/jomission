CLASS: **chance excursion under an uncorrected multiplicity of tests**. Builder defect, counting defect, expected
value or variance defect, and specification ambiguity are each excluded.

| Quantity (rule e_pv_S, unit S) | shape, seed 1 | ref, seed 1 (not gated) |
|---|---|---|

| Gate interval (99.9 %, two-sided) | [417 543, 421 496] | — |
| z | +3.32 | +2.64 |

- Sampler: independent Bernoulli per ordered pair (JaxFNE `connectivity.py` 581-640, called at 1486), so the count
  is exactly Binomial(N_pre·N_post, p). There are no autapses (disjoint populations) and no multi-edges (421 511
  unique pairs). `e_pv_S` is the local rule only. p is density-scaled: K_E 560 · (dens_S / mean dens) / N_E,S
  (`caminos_stack.py` 24, 189-197, 319-329), and it does not depend on N_PV. The gate's mean, variance and interval
  are correct; for pv_pv the gate takes the union of the n² and n(n−1) intervals, which is conservative.
- Dependence, missed in the first diagnostic: with jax 0.10.2 (`threefry_partitionable`), arms at one seed share the
  rule's random stream. The reviewer verified that a 5785×404 uniform draw is the exact prefix of the 5785×518 draw,
  so each arm's count reads the same stream at a different length. Author's check on the JSON: the SD of z across arms
  within a seed is 0.178, and across seeds within an arm 0.919. e_pv_S z for seed 1 over the six arms is 1.67, 2.89,
  2.99, 2.69, 2.68, 3.32, which is one excursion seen six times.
- Effective independent tests: about 130 per stage (5 seeds × 26 rules). One z per stream: SD 0.956, KS p 0.80,
  1 stream beyond the interval against 0.13-0.2 expected. False-stop probability of the frozen gate with a correct
  builder: about 12-18 % per stage. A z of 3.32 here is roughly a 1-in-6 to 1-in-8 event.
- Gap: the gate copies the builder's p formula, so it does not test `local_p` independently. The reviewer checked
  `local_p` separately in this review.
- Not verified: the JaxFNE tile and fold specification beyond `connectivity.py`. The prefix property was checked for
  one tile shape only. The stream structure of the other 25 rule types is inferred from the correlation pattern.

Proposed corrections (owner ruling needed; post hoc after this failure; not implemented):
- A. Keep the gate; the stop stands and stage 2 stays stopped. A rerun with a different engine key would change the
  frozen seeds, so it is not available under the spec.
- B. Keep the 99.9 %-style test and add a family-wise level at 5 %. Over the conservative 780 tests: per-test α
  6.4e-5 (|z| 4.00). Over the about 130 effective streams: α 3.9e-4 (|z| 3.55). Under either, 421 511 passes. B still
