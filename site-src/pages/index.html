Overview

Jomission is a JaxFNE-native computational neurophysiology project for a continuous multi-area laminar visual omission paradigm. The scientific objective is not to manufacture omission responses: it is to determine whether a generically qualified cortical substrate, after structured exposure, develops omission-linked phenotypes, and what mechanisms causally support them.

Gen-1 vs Gen-2

Gen-1 (sealed, immutable) completed canonical exposure and produced predominantly sensory-drive-removal phenotypes. Gen-2 must qualify a generic cortical substrate - persistence, communication, cortical operation, observability - before freeze, and only then ask whether omission physiology emerges. Omission-success outcomes are forbidden calibration targets before freeze.

Evidence classes and statuses

Every panel carries two separate labels. The evidence class says how its content is known; the status says what was concluded. Both come from the project vocabulary, never invented per page.

  • OBSERVED: direct executed outputs.
  • DERIVED: computed from observed content under stated methods.
  • INFERRED: interpretations, labeled as such.
  • ASSUMED / UNKNOWN: explicit assumptions and unknowns.

Statuses include PASS, FAIL, PARTIAL, UNRESOLVED, INFEASIBLE, FEASIBLE, VOID, QUARANTINED, SUPERSEDED, and LOCKED. A sealed negative is class OBSERVED with status FAIL: evidence, not a failure to execute. Retracted and void content is class DERIVED or OBSERVED with status VOID: preserved so errors are not repeated, never reused.
