LIF laminar circuit lab

Leaky integrate-and-fire areas, each with layers L1–L6 of E and I cells at one E:I ratio. Input enters L4 of area 1, output is L6-E of the top area. Feedforward: L5 of area k → L2/3 of area k+1. Feedback: L5 of area k+1 → L1 and L6 of area k. HDP: a hidden gain H per cell and plastic edge gains m, both driven by the cell's dynamics. Sliders apply live; areas, neurons per area, f_I, p, p_FF, p_FB, seed, motor count and cue fraction rebuild the network.
