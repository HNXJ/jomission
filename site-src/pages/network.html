Canonical areas V1, V4, FEF, PFC; layers L1-L6; cell classes E, PV, SST, VIP. Explicit feedforward and feedback motifs with finite delays. Structural connectivity and delays alone do not establish functional hierarchy - see the Propagation Atlas for the cut-controlled verdicts.

TFNE expression

The model is a cortical system in TFNE/2, the sealed specification language: cortical units compose as instances (V1 := CTX[v1]) under declared connection rules (O[fffb] feedforward with feedback, X[lat] lateral). The whole-system construction renders as:

x : {V1^2 X[lat]} O[fffb] {V4^2 X[lat]} O[fffb] {FEF X[lat] PFC} : y

CTX[IZH] and CTX[HH] are alternative realizations of one cortical definition, never different architectures. TFNE is a specification language, not evidence: it names what was built, while the verdicts live in the results.
