## 7. Depth placement, readout only (`scripts/e2_depth_placement.py` -> `results/laminar_e2/depth_placement.json`)

Each ladder run's measured unit cross-spectra are kept; only the readout depths move (zS < zM < zD, 0.05 grid on
-0.6..0.4, 1330 placements, sigma 0.15). Selection: max r_proto among placements with r_ab and r_gm >= 0.6. Own
depths reproduce every verdict file's r values (1e-9 check). Exploratory and post hoc; no model changed.

- Selected on n4000 s1 (S, M, D moved jointly): (-0.25, -0.05, 0.20), r_proto 0.720. Scored unchanged on the 8
  other runs (seed/N held out: same layout family, so this tests seed and N robustness, not generality): r_gm
  0.64-0.84 (8 of 8 >= 0.6; own depths 0.14-0.51), r_proto 0.62-0.74 (own 0.21-0.43), r_ab 0.19-0.61 (1 of 8
  >= 0.6: selecting on r_ab >= 0.6 overfits that band). Coherent r_proto ceiling 0.854.
- Per-run selection puts S at -0.25 in 8 of 9 runs (-0.20 in n1000 s1); M at -0.05 to 0.10, D at 0.20 to 0.40;
  r_proto 0.62-0.75. Placements meeting both band thresholds: 4-65 of 1330 per run.
- S moved alone to -0.25 (M 0, D 0.3 kept): r_gm 0.52-0.81, 8 of 9 >= 0.6 (n4000 s2 0.52); r_proto 0.57-0.75.
- Permutation null (n4000 s1): orders with the S spectra on top reach r_proto 0.740 (S, D, M; above the true
  order) and 0.692 (D, S, M); the three orders with M or D spectra on top meet no placement with both bands
  >= 0.6. The fit does not separate M from D.
- Spectral-shift null (n4000 s1; incoherent spectra, each unit's 1-150 Hz power shape circularly shifted in
  frequency, power kept; 20 draws, same search and selection): unshifted incoherent spectra reach r_proto 0.725
  with 70 joint placements; 8 of 20 draws reach a joint placement, 3 of 20 exceed 0.725 (0.730-0.770) and 4 of 20
  exceed 70 placements (107-207). **The null is not rejected** (about p = 4/21 on r_proto).
- Reading (INFERRED): the joint fit comes mostly from kernel geometry and which unit has the most power at each
  frequency, not from the units' spectral shapes; a unit order with the S unit on top and S read near -0.25 fits
  about as well with shuffled spectral shapes. So this result does not show that the unit spectra carry the
  observed gamma, and it supports H (§2) only weakly. Moving a unit in the model would change its bins,
  density, N and spectra; a sealed depth-layout lineage would be needed to test H.
- Caveat: the search is flexible; r_gm alone cannot order units (a one-unit gamma profile can be placed anywhere,
  `tests/test_e2_depth_placement.py`), so selection is on r_proto with both band thresholds; 20-29% of
  placements reach r_gm >= 0.6 on each run (review), so the 0.6 bar alone is weak.
- Allen replica x0, same sweep on its 4 units (reported by allen-v1-replica, in-sample, 4845 placements;
  `results/allen_v1/scorer/x0_H_s1_T20_readout_sweep_exploratory.json` on its branch): 1203 reach r_gm >= 0.6,
  0 reach both bands. Leave-one-unit-out (unit spectrum replaced by the mean of the other three): joint
  placements 0 (L4, L6), 2 (L2/3), 11 (L5) (`..._leave_one_unit_exploratory.json`). Its shift null on those two
  cases (20 draws): L5 7 of 20 draws >= 11 (p about 0.38), L2/3 5 of 20 >= 2 (p about 0.29); not rejected, so
  the leave-one-out does not identify a unit, and that session withdrew "L5 is the limiting unit"
  (`..._loo_shift_null_exploratory.json`).
- Consensus (both sessions, 2026-11-10): readout-depth sweeps do not locate the r_gm failure in either model;
  no fit recorded here (3-unit joint placement; x0 leave-one-out, second-hand) beats a spectral-shift null at
  20 draws, a weak test.
