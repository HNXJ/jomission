# 11 — MODEL FAILURES & LIMITATIONS (FAILURE ATLAS)

_Atlas of expected biological/phenomenological properties of the Jomission model against the sealed RF×Rate characterization. Each row records a phenomenon the model was expected to exhibit, its verdict (**REPRODUCED** / **CONTRADICTED** / **UNRESOLVED**), the measured evidence, and the **mechanistic constraint** that explains the outcome **without retuning** (i.e., the property follows from the implemented architecture/dynamics as-is). Polarities are frozen: a contradiction is a reproduced-test that came out opposite; unresolved is never rendered as a negative. Sources are the dossier reports R01–R10 and sealed manifests; every number carries its evidence tag._

## 2. Cross-cutting mechanistic constraints (without retuning)

1. **Single-synchronized-rate population.** The per-class spike-count degeneracy (all 242 E units identical whole-trial counts) is the root mechanism behind F-01/F-02/F-06/F-15: there is no firing-rate heterogeneity from which selectivity, enrichment, or heterogeneous balance could emerge (R02 §2b, R01 §3).
2. **Rate-silent plasticity.** Because plasticity (H, w) completes in seconds at reference rate while Δ rate ≈ 0, exposure history is invisible to the firing-rate observable — this simultaneously explains F-08, F-09, and F-10 (Q8 rate NEG) (R04 §5, R05 §6, R07 §1).
3. **Drive-offset, not computation.** The omission response is a bottom-up zero-drive decrement propagating feed-forward through the hierarchy; nothing produces a selective, frontal-predictive, or fixed-timing structure (F-01…F-04, F-07) (R05 §6).
4. **Shared-laminar field proxy.** Near-unity cross-area field correlation is expected from the `proxy_readout` construction (area-attributed partitions of one shared field, depth-only kernel, uncalibrated) — it limits any between-area spectral/timing claim (F-04, F-07; R03 §7).
5. **Two distinct RF-factor realizations.** The RF factor is realized at the config/drive level (RF operator + retinotopic schedule) for all four cells, but cell A's executed hp_hash is the RF-ON canonical — so the "RF off" label for A is not independently verified at the K_HDP level (F-13 caveat; R01 §5, R06 §8). This constrains any RF-on/off contrast built on A.
6. **Execution failure vs negative.** The B/D save crash removes the interaction (F-11) and all phenotype-level factorial estimands (F-12) from measurement. Per the frozen rule, missing execution is `UNRESOLVED`, never `NEGATIVE` (R08 §5, R09 §3).
